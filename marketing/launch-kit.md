## Positioning

**Category:** roster-aware fantasy football draft assistant.

**Promise:** Know who to draft now, who can wait, and where the market price looks wrong.

**Mechanism:** Target Intel combines the available player pool, roster settings, current picks, ADP price, VBD, positional scarcity and next-turn availability.

**Primary offer:** one live pick free, then $6.99 for one complete draft. No recurring subscription.

**Audience:** a manager drafting a paid redraft league in the next 7–21 days who already uses rankings or ADP but wants help making live choices.

Avoid leading with “AI,” CatBoost, CQR or the number of model features. Use the model details as proof after the buyer understands the live decision.

## Daily content rotation

Post one proof/demo item and one player/decision item each day. Do not attach a sales link to every reply.

1. **Model vs market:** one player with model positional rank, ADP positional rank and the price implication.
2. **Take now or wait:** record a 15-second screen capture showing Target Intel before and after a pick.
3. **Roster reaction:** draft three players at one position and show how the next recommendation changes.
4. **Risk range:** show two similarly projected players with different interval widths.
5. **Honest limitation:** explain one thing the model cannot predict and how a manager should account for it.
6. **Draft-room question:** ask followers to choose between two players, then reply later with the model's decision and reasoning.
7. **Board update:** list the three largest model-vs-ADP changes after each data refresh.
