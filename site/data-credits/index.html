nflverse

OverADP uses selected data from nflverse-data, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

OverADP filters, normalizes, joins, and derives features from the source data. The League Intel view uses public player and team statistics, injury-report history, and schedules. These are not current medical clearances or guaranteed future results.

nflverse does not sponsor or endorse OverADP.

Yahoo Fantasy

When you connect your account, Yahoo Fantasy supplies your current league and roster information for a private, read-only session. The public nflverse context is matched to it in your browser. Yahoo league data is used only for your session and is not stored.

Projections

Team Hub projections blend OverADP's v6 preseason model with each player's results and usage this season. The blend was calibrated and tested on held-out 2019–2025 seasons using public nflverse data. They are estimates, not guarantees.
