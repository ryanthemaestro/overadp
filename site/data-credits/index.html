nflverse

OverADP uses selected data from nflverse-data, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

OverADP filters, normalizes, joins, and derives features from the source data. The League Intel view uses public player and team statistics, injury-report history, and schedules. These are not current medical clearances or guaranteed future results.

nflverse does not sponsor or endorse OverADP.

Yahoo Fantasy

When you connect your account, Yahoo Fantasy supplies your current league and roster information for a private, read-only session. The public nflverse context is matched to it in your browser. Yahoo league data is not sent to Jev.

Jev

Jev's independent, experimental matchup review uses only the public nflverse snapshot. It is not a Yahoo-informed lineup recommendation. When evidence is thin, the review can return “uncertain.”
