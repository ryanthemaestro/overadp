Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

This policy explains what OverADP collects when you browse the site, create an account, use the Draft War Room, or make a purchase.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, and authentication information needed to keep you signed in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not available to OverADP in plain text.
  • Draft activity: your selected players, players marked taken, league settings, and draft-access status so a paid draft can be resumed.
  • Purchase information: payment status, amount, currency, Stripe checkout identifier, and purchase time. Stripe processes card details; OverADP does not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Usage and attribution information: page views, feature events, device/browser information, and campaign parameters such as UTM tags or Google click identifiers.
  • Support messages: information you choose to send when requesting help.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and paid access, save and restore draft progress, process and verify purchases, provide support, prevent abuse, understand the funnel, improve OverADP, and meet applicable obligations.

Service providers

OverADP uses service providers including Netlify for hosting, Supabase for accounts and stored access state, Stripe for payments, and Google Analytics for site measurement. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser storage for sign-in tokens, draft progress, settings, and attribution continuity. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technology. Blocking storage may prevent account, draft-resume, or measurement features from working correctly.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, support purchases, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and maintain required records. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of account information by emailing overadp@gmail.com. Some purchase or security records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required. You can also control cookies and local storage through your browser.

Children

OverADP is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will identify the latest published version.

Contact

Questions may be sent to overadp@gmail.com.
