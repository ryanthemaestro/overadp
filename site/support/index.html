How can we help?

Email support

Send your question directly to:

overadp@gmail.com

We aim to respond within two business days.

What to include

  • The email address associated with your OverADP account
  • A short description of what happened
  • Your browser and device, plus a screenshot when useful

Never email passwords, full payment-card details, or fantasy-platform login credentials. OverADP support will not ask for them.
