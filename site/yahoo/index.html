How the weekly advice works

Early-season estimates blend last season's active-game scoring average with this season's completed games under your Yahoo league rules. With only one or two current games, this is a rough heuristic, not a validated projection. A missing score is not zero. Defense/special teams may lack public scoring categories and will remain unranked.

The browser finds the highest-scoring eligible lineup from players with verified upcoming kickoffs, while keeping already-locked starters in place.
