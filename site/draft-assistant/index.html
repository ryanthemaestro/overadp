Questions before kickoff

Know exactly what you are getting

What does the 50.6% top-three result mean?

It is the regular-season top-three rate for Target Intel teams in 1,000 paired retrospective simulations using 2023 and 2024 true ADP. The comparison held the Week-1 lineup fixed to isolate the roster created at the draft. It is encouraging historical evidence, not a promise of future results or prize money.

Why not just follow ADP?

ADP measures market behavior: where players are usually selected. OverADP uses ADP as a price signal, then adds its own projections, roster fit, positional value and next-turn availability to make a decision for your specific draft.

Does OverADP connect directly to ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper?

Not currently. You mark your picks and opponent picks as the draft proceeds. That makes the War Room platform-independent, but it requires manual input while direct league integrations are developed.

Does OverADP guarantee a winning draft?

No. Injuries, weekly management, waivers, trades and playoff variance all matter. OverADP is a transparent draft decision aid, not a guarantee of a particular finish.

What formats does it support?

The War Room supports configurable team counts, draft slots, roster settings and common scoring formats. The published 2026 board defaults to half-PPR.

Can I try it before creating an account?

Yes. Mark live-board picks taken, watch Target Intel recalculate and record one pick free. An account is required only if you choose the $6.99 full-draft unlock.
