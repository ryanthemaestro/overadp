Questions before kickoff

What to expect

What makes OverADP an AI fantasy football draft assistant?

OverADP uses tuned position-specific machine-learning models for QB, RB, WR and TE, then combines their projections with your roster, league settings, live picks, VBD, ADP and next-turn availability. It is a transparent draft decision tool, not a generic chatbot.

Does OverADP connect directly to ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper?

Not currently. You mark your picks and opponent picks in the War Room as the draft proceeds. That keeps it platform-independent but requires manual input.

Does it guarantee a winning draft?

No projection can guarantee an individual outcome. OverADP is a decision aid: it makes the model, uncertainty and draft-price tradeoff visible while you choose.

What formats does it support?

The War Room supports configurable team counts, draft slots, roster settings and common scoring formats. The published 2026 board defaults to half-PPR.

How was the projection model tested?

The published results aggregate 1,052 player-season predictions from chronological 2024 and 2025 test folds. The full process and limitations are public on the methodology page.

Yes. Make five roster-aware picks free. An account is required only when you choose a paid unlock.
