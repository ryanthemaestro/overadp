How to read this table

Proj Pts: half-PPR fantasy points the point model estimates over the full 2026 season. 80% CI: a split-conformal range with an 80% marginal-coverage target, calibrated on 2025 rows. A wide range means more uncertainty. ADP: current half-PPR average draft position. Risk: relative interval width bucketed within position.

How the results were measured

The point model was trained only on earlier seasons and evaluated on the next season. The published metrics aggregate every eligible player row from the 2024 and 2025 test folds. An exact-cohort ADP-only check is directionally positive, but we withhold a market-beating percentage because the 2025 input is a preseason-rank proxy rather than true ADP.

The biggest gaps between model and ADP are surfaced on our Top Sleepers and Top Busts pages. The full War Room shows every player with filtering, VBD, scarcity, and draft-tracking.
